# Acme Corp Employee Handbook (2026)

## Remote Work Policy

Acme Corp is a fully remote company.

- All employees may work from any location in their registered country.
- Core collaboration hours are **10:00–14:00** in your local timezone.
- Cameras-on is required for all-hands meetings and optional for 1:1s.

---

## Time Off

- Unlimited PTO with a mandatory **15-day minimum** per year.
- Sick leave is uncapped and does not require a doctor's note for the first **5 days**.
- Parental leave:
  - **16 weeks fully paid** for primary caregivers.
  - **8 weeks fully paid** for secondary caregivers.

---

## Expense Reimbursement

- Submit expenses within **30 days** via the Expensify integration.
- Hardware budget:
  - **$2,500** every **3 years** for laptops.
  - **$500** for peripherals.
- Home office stipend:
  - **$1,000** one-time for new hires.
- Travel must be pre-approved by your manager for amounts over **$500**.

---

## Performance Reviews

Reviews happen **twice per year**:

- January
- July

### Review Process

- Self-assessment
- Peer feedback

### Calibration

- Calibration meetings happen the week after review submissions close.

### Promotions

- Promotions are considered in the **July cycle only**.

---

## Security

- Hardware MFA (**YubiKey**) is required for all production system access.
- Secrets must never be committed to Git.
  - Use the company **1Password vault** instead.
- Report phishing attempts to:

```text
security@acme.com
```
